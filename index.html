Learning with purpose. Building with possibility.

Established in 2007 by the Balaram Panda Trust, GIFT Autonomous is an institution in Bhubaneswar offering education in engineering, computing, management and research. The institution promotes academic learning, research, innovation and professional development.

Across classrooms, laboratories and student communities, the emphasis is on learning by doing and finding room to grow.
