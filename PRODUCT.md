# GIFT Autonomous concept preview

## Purpose
An unofficial, public concept preview of a one-day frontend proposal by Quadvexa for a GIFT management meeting. It demonstrates a possible visual direction and a few navigable workflows, not a commissioned institutional website.

## Audience and tasks
GIFT leadership, faculty and IT review the proposed homepage, mobile navigation, programme discovery, CSE content and a simulated notice workflow. Prospective students are represented only as a design audience; no real applicant data is handled.

## Platform and constraints
Responsive local web app in Next.js, React, TypeScript and Tailwind. Source content and media are local. No backend, authentication, submissions, deployment or production integrations. Demo notices are fictitious and browser-local. Official external destinations are visibly labelled.

## Evidence and open decisions
Facts and assets are drawn from the supplied public-source resource pack; claims requiring institutional confirmation are omitted or labelled. Public use of GIFT branding and imagery requires permission. Current programme availability, accreditation, placement data and faculty details remain unverified for this concept.
